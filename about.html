Our Story

About CoolBreeze Air Services

Twelve years of principled, credential-forward HVAC practice — serving the Klang Valley with honesty and technical excellence.

Who We Are

Built on Expertise, Delivered with Integrity

CoolBreeze Air Services was founded in 2013 by a team of certified HVAC engineers who believed the Klang Valley deserved a contractor that combined technical rigour with genuine transparency. From our base in Petaling Jaya, we have grown from a three-man team to a 20-strong operation — but our principles have not changed.

Every project we take on — whether a single bedroom unit or a multi-floor VRF system — receives the same diligence: proper load calculations, compliant installation, and a written performance report. We do not cut corners, and we do not recommend work that is not needed.

Our 98% customer satisfaction rate across more than 850 completed projects reflects not just technical competence, but a commitment to treating every client's property with the respect it deserves.
