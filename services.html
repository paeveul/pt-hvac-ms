Our HVAC Services

From a single wall-mounted unit to a full building VRF system, CoolBreeze delivers end-to-end air-conditioning solutions backed by certified expertise.

AC Installation

Our technicians are factory-trained and certified to handle everything from a single bedroom unit to multi-split systems across a full property. Every installation includes proper piping, cabling, drainage, and a post-installation performance check.

  • Residential single-split and multi-split systems
  • Light commercial cassette and ceiling units
  • All brands and inverter models
  • Proper drainage and electrical compliance
  • Post-installation commissioning and test run

AC Servicing & Maintenance

Regular servicing is the single most effective way to extend your AC unit's lifespan and keep energy bills low. CoolBreeze offers one-time servicing and annual maintenance contracts for both residential and commercial clients. Each visit covers full cleaning, filter washing, coil inspection, gas pressure check, and a written service report.

  • Filter cleaning and sanitisation
  • Evaporator and condenser coil wash
  • Refrigerant gas level check
  • Drainage pan and pipe inspection
  • Annual contract plans available

AC Repair

When your unit stops cooling, leaks, or trips your electrical switch, you need a fast, accurate diagnosis. Our technicians carry a full set of tools and common spare parts on every callout, minimising downtime. We provide transparent repair cost estimates before any work begins.

  • Gas leak detection and top-up
  • Compressor and fan motor replacement
  • PCB and electrical fault repair
  • Water leak and drainage blockage
  • Remote control and sensor issues

  • New builds and retrofits
  • Residential bungalows and commercial fit-outs

VRV / VRF Systems

Variable Refrigerant Volume/Flow (VRV/VRF) systems are the gold standard for large-scale commercial HVAC. CoolBreeze is experienced in designing and commissioning Daikin VRV and Mitsubishi City Multi systems for office buildings, hotels, hospitals, and factories — with full integration into BMS where required.

  • Load calculation and system design
  • Multi-zone simultaneous heating and cooling
  • Daikin VRV and Mitsubishi City Multi
  • BMS integration and remote monitoring
  • Energy performance reporting
